Buyer guide

How to compare fashion deals

Compare clothing and accessory offers by exact size, color, material, pack quantity, condition, and return policy. A discount is useful only when the item and fit match what you need.

Use the merchant size chart

Sizing varies by brand and product line. Compare measurements rather than relying only on a familiar letter size.

Check the exact variant

Price can change by color, size, material, or pack quantity. Confirm the selected variant before checkout.

Review return terms

Final-sale rules, return shipping, restocking fees, and condition requirements can outweigh a modest discount.
