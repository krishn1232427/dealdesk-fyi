Buyer guide

How to compare streaming deals

Compare listed video, channel, documentary, and music-streaming offers with the trial length, monthly price, eligibility, and renewal terms shown together. Catalogs and plan features can change, so the merchant page remains the source of truth.

Price after the trial

A free trial is temporary. Check the regular monthly or annual rate and set a reminder before the automatic renewal date if you are still deciding.

Plan and device limits

Compare ads, simultaneous streams, offline viewing, resolution, supported devices, and whether the offer requires another membership.

Content availability

A low price is useful only when the service carries what you want to watch or hear. Confirm the current catalog and regional availability before subscribing.
