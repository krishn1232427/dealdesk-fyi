Buyer guide

How to compare business and industrial deals

Browse equipment, components, storage, and industrial listings by part number, specification, condition, and delivered price. Compatibility and freight terms often matter more than a broad category discount.

Verify the part number

Confirm manufacturer, model, revision, voltage, interface, capacity, dimensions, and compatibility with the intended system.

Understand operating condition

Used, refurbished, surplus, and untested equipment carry different risks. Review testing notes, warranty, hours, and included components.

Calculate delivered cost

Freight, lift-gate service, duties, installation, calibration, and return shipping can change the final economics.
