Buyer guide

How to compare grocery deals

Compare grocery offers by package count, weight or volume, unit price, dietary details, and subscription requirements. The lowest package price is not always the lowest cost per serving or item.

Compare the unit price

Divide the displayed price by count, weight, volume, or serving so different pack sizes can be compared fairly.

Confirm variant and ingredients

Flavor, formula, allergens, dietary certifications, and package size may differ between otherwise similar listings.

Check delivery and recurring terms

Verify delivery dates, temperature-sensitive shipping, expiration guidance, and whether the price requires recurring delivery.
