How to compare home deals

Compare listed home-product offers with model details, displayed savings, shipping terms, and verification dates. For appliances and smart-home equipment, warranty coverage and the exact bundle can matter as much as the headline discount.

Compare the exact bundle

Review delivery and returns

Large or heavy items can carry delivery restrictions or return costs. Verify the final shipping and return terms at checkout.
