DealDesk

Only real deals. Only links that pay DealDesk.

DealDesk verifies the offer, the destination, and our commission eligibility before a deal appears. Shoppers get a clear path to the merchant; DealDesk gets paid only when the merchant or affiliate network records a qualifying action.

View on the App Store

How it works

Review stays native. Buy goes to the merchant.

Review opens a DealDesk decision sheet with price, coupon, shipping, seller, evidence, and risk signals. Buy opens the merchant item page so the customer can confirm checkout details directly with the merchant.

Decision Tools

Save buying plans, compare candidates, watch prices, estimate checkout totals, review coupon status, and export decision summaries.

Affiliate Disclosure

DealDesk may earn a commission from some purchases when users choose to open merchant purchase links. The app shows this disclosure near Buy actions.

Only approved, commission-confirmed merchant links appear in the public deal feeds.

Customer Control

The merchant cart remains the source of truth for final price, taxes, shipping, availability, return terms, and checkout conditions.

Publisher

DealDesk is published by Launchdesk LLC. The app does not use adware, browser extensions, cookie stuffing, hidden link replacement, or misleading purchase flows.

Commission-confirmed

Today’s verified product deals.

Every visible deal has an approved network, an active DealDesk tracking ID, and a recently checked offer. Price and availability can still change at merchant checkout.

Streaming deals

Subscription offers that can earn DealDesk a bounty.

These links use the approved Amazon Associates tracking ID dealdesk-20. A commission is earned only when the visitor completes the qualifying signup or purchase.

Publishing rule

Unapproved partners stay off the deal pages.

Awin, CJ, Rakuten Advertising, Impact, and Sovrn offers remain private until DealDesk is approved for the advertiser and has a tested tracked link. This prevents attractive but non-paying offers from entering the public feed.
