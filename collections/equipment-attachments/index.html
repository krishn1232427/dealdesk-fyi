Verify fitment

Confirm mount pattern, shaft, hydraulic flow and pressure, dimensions, capacity, machine weight, and required adapters.

Inspect included components

Calculate freight and returns

Check delivery method, lift-gate needs, commercial-address rules, damage inspection, installation, and return freight.
